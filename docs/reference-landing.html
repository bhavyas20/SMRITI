How it works

Three small things a day. One quiet answer for you.

Smriti sits in the background of your parent's home — a tablet or their own phone — and asks for very little. What it gathers, it turns into something you can read in half a minute.

Inside Smriti

Four things you'll actually use

01 · Daily check-ins

A quiet "good morning" that tells you a lot.

Sleep, appetite, mood — three taps at her own pace, and a note in your day by nine.

02 · Gentle reminders

Medicine, remembered — without the nagging.

A soft chime at her hour, in her language. If a dose is missed twice, you're the one who hears about it.

03 · The weekly report

The week, gathered for everyone who cares.

One page every Sunday: routines kept, what changed, what to ask her doctor. Shared with the siblings, so nobody's guessing.

04 · A memory a day

One small story, shared with the family.

Smriti asks her about a photo, a place, a song. What she says becomes something your children will still have.
